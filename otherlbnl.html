Selected Applied Science Projects

A selection of UX design work across scientific software, including data exploration, publishing workflows, computational tools, and research infrastructure.

Climate resilience

WELDER

WELDER enables facility managers and policy makers to model how extreme weather events might reprioritize maintenance schedules for facilities and buildings. The Climate Recon app visualizes how extreme weather events are projected to change over time.

Scientific data

ESS-DIVE

The Environmental Systems Science Data Infrastructure for a Virtual Ecosystem (ESS-DIVE) is a repository for Earth and environmental science data. This concept explores a streamlined workflow for publishing scientific data packages.

Research workflows

Science Capsule

Science Capsule helps scientists capture and manage next-generation knowledge discovery workflows. Shown here as a JupyterLab extension integrated with a notebook, the app captures data-generation events alongside other research processes.

Decision support

PARETO

PARETO supports environmentally sustainable approaches to managing, treating, and beneficially reusing water produced from oil and gas operations. This concept explores workflows and interface components that support planning and reuse decisions.
